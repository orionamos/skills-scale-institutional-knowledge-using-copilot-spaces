# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Receive technical guidance on design decisions and architecture; seek approval on complex implementations
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test strategy and ensure code is testable; review and address quality feedback
- **With Product Manager**: Clarify acceptance criteria and validate feature understanding; provide implementation estimates
- **With Project Manager**: Report progress and identify blockers; participate in sprint planning and retrospectives

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Owner**: Align on backlog priorities and feature requirements; ensure stakeholder needs are captured
- **With Developers**: Define acceptance criteria clearly; gather estimates for capacity planning
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Define quality standards and acceptance criteria; review test plans
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Present roadmap updates and business impact; seek approval on prioritization trade-offs
- **With UX/Design Lead**: Collaborate on user-centered design and feature validation; review design mockups and usability

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Scrum Master/Agile Coach**: Align on iteration planning and process improvements; collaborate on removing team blockers
- **With Engineering Manager**: Coordinate on resource allocation and team capacity; escalate resource constraints
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Report progress against milestones; flag risks and dependency issues
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Track technical risks and dependencies; ensure architectural work is reflected in plan
- **With all team members**: Facilitate standups, planning, and retrospectives; communicate status and timeline changes

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy and testing approach for the project. They collaborate with product and engineering to define acceptance criteria, design test plans, and validate that deliverables meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and QA approach (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance, security)
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Lead manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Validate release readiness through smoke tests and regression testing
- Collaborate with developers on test coverage and CI/CD improvements

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet user expectations
- Reduce defects escaping to production
- Maintain fast feedback loops through automated testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (define testability)
- QA standups and test execution status
- Bug triage and severity assessment
- Release readiness reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Define testability requirements early; collaborate on test coverage and CI/CD enhancements; review code for quality issues
- **With Product Manager**: Clarify acceptance criteria; validate feature specifications; provide quality risk assessments
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Review technical design for testability; ensure testing strategy aligns with architecture
- **With Project Manager**: Report test progress and quality metrics; flag quality-related risks and delays

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical vision and architecture guidance. They make critical design decisions, oversee system integration, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for features
- Review design proposals and ensure alignment with system standards
- Guide trade-offs between features, performance, and maintainability
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Mentor developers and promote best practices
- Ensure integration with other systems and APIs

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Enable team to execute efficiently with clear technical direction

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews for complex or cross-system changes
- Sprint planning for technical feasibility assessment
- Risk escalations for technical challenges

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide architectural guidance; review complex code changes; mentor on technical best practices
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Review design for testability; collaborate on integration and end-to-end testing strategies
- **With Product Manager**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs; validate architectural alignment with product roadmap
- **With Project Manager**: Escalate technical risks and dependencies; inform timeline estimates based on architectural complexity
- **With Engineering Manager**: Collaborate on team skill development; review technical roadmap alignment

---

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
Product Owners manage backlog prioritization and translate stakeholder requirements into actionable user stories. They work closely with Product Managers and the development team to ensure features deliver measurable business value.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and prioritize the product backlog
- Write clear, testable user stories with acceptance criteria
- Represent stakeholder voice in development decisions
- Conduct stakeholder interviews to understand requirements
- Validate that delivered features meet business objectives
- Manage scope and prevent feature creep

### Goals
- Deliver features that maximize stakeholder satisfaction
- Keep backlog well-organized and prioritized
- Reduce rework and ensure clarity of requirements

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Stakeholder interviews and feedback collection
- User story workshops with development team
- Sprint reviews and acceptance validation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager**: Align on vision and priorities; ensure backlog reflects product strategy
- **With Developers**: Clarify requirements; answer questions on user stories; validate implementation against acceptance criteria
- **With Stakeholder/Sponsor**: Gather requirements and feedback; communicate feature progress and delivery status
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Refine acceptance criteria; define quality expectations; validate feature acceptance

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes and coach teams on agile best practices. They remove blockers, protect team focus, and foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, daily standup, retrospectives, reviews)
- Remove impediments and blockers that hinder team progress
- Coach team on agile principles and practices
- Track team velocity and sprint metrics
- Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback
- Support team in continuous process improvement

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Maintain consistent sprint cadence and delivery rhythm
- Build a culture of learning and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching with team members
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Metrics reporting and process improvement discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinate on sprint ceremonies and timeline management; collaborate on risk escalation
- **With all team members**: Remove blockers; facilitate communication; coach on agile practices; support collaboration
- **With Engineering Manager**: Report on team health and velocity; escalate people-related concerns; support talent development

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers oversee team capacity, career development, and resource allocation. They ensure team members have the support and growth opportunities they need while balancing project commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity and resource allocation
- Support career development and skill growth
- Conduct performance reviews and feedback
- Hire and onboard new team members
- Escalate resource constraints and staffing needs
- Foster team culture and psychological safety

### Goals
- Build high-performing, engaged teams
- Develop talent and reduce turnover
- Balance team workload and prevent burnout

### Typical Communication
- One-on-one regular check-ins with team members
- Resource planning meetings
- Hiring and onboarding coordination
- Career development and performance reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinate on resource allocation; discuss team capacity and constraints
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Align on skill development needs and technical mentorship
- **With Scrum Master/Agile Coach**: Monitor team health and velocity; address process improvements
- **With all developers**: Support career growth; provide feedback and mentorship; manage workload and well-being

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business direction, secure resources, and provide final approval on project decisions. They represent business, customer, or organizational interests and ensure projects align with strategic goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Provide approval and sign-off on major decisions
- Secure resources and budget for projects
- Communicate project importance to broader organization
- Escalate and resolve cross-team conflicts
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy

### Goals
- Maximize business value from projects
- Ensure strategic alignment and risk mitigation
- Enable team success through clear direction and support

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and kickoff meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gates and approval meetings
- Risk escalation and incident communication

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager**: Align on vision and priorities; provide business context and requirements
- **With Project Manager**: Review project status and milestones; approve scope changes and timeline adjustments
- **With Product Owner**: Provide requirements and feedback; validate feature delivery against business objectives
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Review technical approach for strategic alignment; approve architectural decisions with business impact

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience standards and design practices. They ensure features are usable, accessible, and aligned with user needs and brand standards through research, design, and iterative validation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas
- Design user interfaces and interactions
- Establish design systems and standards
- Lead design reviews and provide feedback
- Validate designs through user testing
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design practices

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centered products
- Maintain design consistency across features
- Reduce user friction and support adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design workshops and user research sessions
- Design review meetings with stakeholders and engineering
- User testing and feedback collection
- Design system documentation and guidelines

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager**: Collaborate on feature definition and user needs; present design research and user insights
- **With Developers**: Review implementation for design fidelity; provide design specifications and assets; iterate on user feedback
- **With Product Owner**: Clarify user experience requirements in acceptance criteria; validate final designs
- **With QA/Testing Lead**: Include usability and accessibility in test plans; collaborate on user testing
